Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. This draft explains the information handled by the Digimarketingsolutions website.

Who we are

Digimarketingsolutions is a digital marketing service based near Ameerpet, Hyderabad. For privacy questions or requests, contact digimarketingsolutions16@gmail.com or call +91 6281312546.

Information we handle

  • Contact enquiries. Name, email, optional phone number, selected service and message. The website API also records the time and request IP address for its enquiry backup.
  • Chat messages. Messages sent to the website assistant may be processed by Anthropic through our server when AI chat is configured. The chat also keeps a conversation copy in your browser session storage.
  • Service cart. The cart stores selected service identifiers and quantities in your browser’s local storage. It does not collect card or bank details.

How information is used and shared

We use enquiries to respond to you and discuss requested services. Contact-form information is sent through the form delivery provider configured on the site and is also submitted to our website API for an enquiry backup. Chat messages are sent to the AI provider only when AI chat is configured. These providers process data to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

We do not offer online payment on this website and do not request payment-card details here. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and website.

Storage, retention and your choices

Enquiry records are kept only as long as needed to respond, manage a client relationship, meet legal obligations or resolve disputes. Browser cart data remains until you clear it or remove the items. Chat session data is stored in the browser session and may be cleared by closing the session or clearing site data.

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information by emailing us. We will review the request and respond subject to applicable legal requirements. You can also clear local and session storage through your browser settings.

Security and updates

We use reasonable measures to protect enquiries but cannot guarantee that internet transmission or storage is completely secure. We may update this notice when website features or data practices change; the revised date will appear on this page.

This website uses browser storage for its cart and chat, rather than advertising cookies. If analytics or other tracking tools are added, this notice and any required consent controls should be updated.
