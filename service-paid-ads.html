Overview

Paid advertising puts your offer in front of people exactly when they are interested. Unlike organic channels, you can switch it on quickly, measure every rupee and scale what performs.

Our media buyers build campaigns around your target cost per lead, test creatives constantly and report honestly, so your budget goes to what actually converts.

Our process

  1. Goals and budget

    We agree target cost per lead and monthly spend.

  2. Set up and tracking

    Accounts, pixels and conversion events are configured.

  3. Launch

    Campaigns go live with multiple ads and audiences.

  4. Optimise weekly

    We cut what does not work and scale what does.

Results you can expect

  • Qualified leads within the first weeks
  • Lower cost per lead through weekly optimisation
  • Clear return on ad spend reporting
  • Retargeting that brings visitors back to buy

Tools we use: Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, Google Tag Manager, GA4, Looker Studio

Frequently asked questions

What budget do I need?

We recommend starting with a test budget that fits your market. We will suggest a figure after your free audit.

Is the ad spend included in your fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Google or Meta from your account, so you keep full control and transparency.

How fast will I get leads?

Paid ads can bring leads within days, with performance improving as we learn your audience.

Do I own the ad accounts?

Yes. All accounts and data stay in your name.
